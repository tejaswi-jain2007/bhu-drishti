# Brag Plan: भू-DRISHTI (eRTMAC-NWIS)

## What is this app?
An AI/ML-driven decision-support and institutional intelligence platform for Oil India Limited (SIH 2026) that transforms historical offset well completion reports into depth-indexed real-time drilling hazard predictions.

## The angle
Deep subsurface intelligence meets real-time drilling de-risking. Show how decades of complex drilling records across India's sedimentary basins are instantly analyzed to predict downhole hazards before spudding.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"DRILLING AT 3,500m IN GLOOMY BARRAIL FORMATION? PROBABLY A MUD LOSS OR KICK ZONE."
Rapid zoom into a 2.5D geodetic GIS map of Assam Basin with active well pins pulsing.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Interactive Geodetic Map & Basin Explorer**: Real-time Haversine offset well discovery across 8 exploration provinces.
2. **Depth-Wise Multi-Factor Risk Engine**: AI forecasting hazard probabilities (mud loss, stuck pipe, kick influx) indexed by depth.
3. **Zero-Hallucination RAG Assistant**: Instant provenance-backed answers pulling exact WCR/DDR document pages and evidence text.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Select Target Location**: User picks Assam / Tinsukia district and inputs planned depth (4,200m).
2. **Execute Multi-Well Intelligence Pipeline**: Spatial search identifies 7 offset wells and calculates stratigraphy correlation.
3. **Review Hazard Advisory & Proven Mitigations**: System highlights historical pipe freeing pills and casing seat recommendations.
